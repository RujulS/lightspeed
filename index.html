    How this works

    Planets sit at their real positions for the simulated date (JPL orbital elements) and spin at their IAU rotation rates. Voyager-class trips inside the Solar System fly real two-body transfer orbits with a launch-window search.

    Fast ships follow exact special-relativistic motion: constant acceleration, coasting, flip-and-burn braking. Ship clocks run slow.

    What you see at high speed is physical. Starlight crowds toward the direction of travel and stars ahead turn bluer and brighter. At extreme speed the Big Bang's afterglow ahead shifts into visible light.

    Stars: 109,000 real stars at their 3-D positions. Gaia BH1 bends light by exact Schwarzschild ray tracing. The real Gaia BH1 is dormant; its glowing disk is an optional "what if it were feeding" view.
